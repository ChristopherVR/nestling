# Backups and updates

Your log lives on your own devices, so keeping a copy is up to you. If you share with a partner, their phone already has a full copy. A backup file is still a good idea.

## Export a backup

<div class="steps">

1. Open **History → ⋮ menu**, or **Settings → Your data**.
2. Tap **Export backup** and choose where to save the `.json` file.
3. Keep it somewhere safe, such as your cloud drive or email.

</div>

## Import a backup

Choose **Import backup** in the same menu and pick the file. Importing **adds and updates**; it never deletes anything, so importing the same file twice is harmless.

This is also how you move to a new phone without a family hub: export on the old phone, import on the new one.

## Export to a spreadsheet

**Export CSV** saves the entries that History currently shows as a spreadsheet. Filter History first to export only a day or a date range.

## Updating Nestling

- **Google Play:** updates come through Google Play.
- **APK from GitHub:** tap **Settings → This device → App version**, then **Update**. Nestling checks the download before installing it.
- **Windows:** close Nestling and extract the new ZIP into a new folder. Your log carries over.

Updates keep your log. After an update, Nestling shows **What's new** once. All release notes are on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/ChristopherVR/nestling/releases).

::: tip Update every device
Phones on different versions pause syncing until they match. Update all devices in your family hub around the same time.
:::
