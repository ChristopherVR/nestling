# Logging basics

Everything starts on **Today**. The big cards show how long it has been since the last feed and the last nappy, and the buttons below log the next one.

## Feeds

<div class="steps">

1. Tap **Feed** when a feed starts.
2. Pick **Left**, **Right**, **Both** or **Bottle**. For breastfeeding, Nestling suggests which side is next.
3. Tap **Start timer**, or just pick a side and save if you are logging afterwards.
4. Need to burp? Tap **Pause to burp**. The timer stops counting until you tap **Resume feeding**.
5. Tap **Stop & save**. Only the actual feeding time is saved, not the breaks.

</div>

A running or paused timer survives closing the app. Turn on **Every 5 minutes** for a gentle chime after each five minutes of feeding.

## Nappies

Tap **Nappy**, then **Pee**, **Poo** or **Pee + Poo**. That's it: one tap. Today shows how long it has been since the last change.

## Sleep

Tap **Start nap** when you put your baby down, and **End nap** when they wake. You can adjust the start time.

If you open Feed or Nappy during a nap, Nestling asks whether the nap has finished. Choose **Still sleeping** to keep it running while you log the feed or change.

## Growth

Open **Growth** and tap **Add a moment**. Add any of these, for example at each clinic visit:

- weight in kg
- height in cm
- a photo
- a short memory

The journal groups moments by month, shows your baby's age, and charts weight and height by date. These are your own measurements, not medical percentiles.

## Routines

Open **Routines** to set up bath time, bedtime, tummy time or anything else. Choose a time, the weekdays and optional notes.

- Tap **Mark done** to record who did it and when.
- Turn on **Remind me on this device** for a weekly reminder on the chosen days.

## More than one baby

Use the baby selector at the top of Today to switch between babies. The person‑plus button adds a baby; the pencil changes their name, birthday or photo. Feeds, nappies, sleep and routines belong to the baby that is selected.

## Fixing a mistake

Open **History** and tap any entry to edit or delete it. Deleted something by accident? Tap **Undo**.

## History and charts

**History** is your activity log. Search it, filter by type, or pick a day, a date range or even one hour of each day. Use the day arrows to step through dates.

Tap **Charts** for daily totals (feeds, wees, poos, sleep, feeding minutes, bottle volume) or hourly patterns. Tap a bar for details.

::: tip Night mode
From 6 pm to 6 am the whole app switches to its dark colours by itself, so it is easier on your eyes during night feeds. A small moon shows when this is on.
:::
