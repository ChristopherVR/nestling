# Momcozy breast pump

Nestling can connect to supported Momcozy wearable pumps over Bluetooth. Control them from your phone and save each session with how long it took and how much you pumped, next to your feeds.

::: info Before you start
- Pump control works on **Android** only. You can still log pumping by hand on any device.
- Supported models: **M5 Pro (BP380)**, **M9**, **M9 Pro**, **M10** and **Air 1**. Testing so far has been on the M5 Pro.
- This is an unofficial integration. It is not made or endorsed by Momcozy.
:::

## Connect your pumps

<div class="steps">

1. On Today, tap **Pump**. (Don't see it? Turn on **Show Pump on Today** in Settings.)
2. Switch the pumps on, and **close the Momcozy app**: a pump talks to one app at a time.
3. Tap **Find pumps**. When Android asks for **Nearby devices**, allow it, and make sure Bluetooth is on.
4. Tap your pump in **Pumps nearby** to connect. If Nestling cannot tell which model it is, it asks you to pick it.

</div>

Nestling remembers your pumps, so next time they show under **Your pumps**.

## Pump

- Tap **Pump now**. With two pumps, choose **Both together** to run them as a pair.
- Pick a mode: **Massage**, **Expression** or **Mixed**, and use **Lower** / **Higher** to set the suction level.
- **Pause** and **Start** as you need.
- Tap **Finish and save**. Then **Add how much you pumped**: the M5 Pro cannot measure milk, so enter the amount yourself.

Prefer not to connect? Choose **Log by hand** to save a session with its time, side and amount.

## Sessions from the pump

Sessions you ran without Nestling can be read from the pump. Open the pump and tap **Check for sessions on the pump**, then **Add all** to save them.

::: warning Nestling and the Momcozy app share this history
Once a pump's history has been read, it is removed from the pump. Whichever app reads it first gets it.
:::

## Stats

The Pump page shows recent sessions and totals per day, and **Charts** includes pumping sessions, minutes and millilitres per day.

## Firmware updates

Nestling shows each pump's firmware version but does not update it. Install updates with the Momcozy app, then check in Nestling that both pumps match.

## Trouble connecting?

- Switch the pump off and on, close the Momcozy app, and tap **Look again**.
- Check that Bluetooth is on and Nestling has the **Nearby devices** permission.
- Still stuck? **Trouble connecting? Open the pump lab** shows diagnostics you can include in a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/ChristopherVR/nestling/issues).
