# Reminders

Nestling can remind you when the next feed is due, when it is time for your pills, and when a routine comes up. Reminders are set **per phone**: your partner chooses their own.

## Feed reminder

<div class="steps">

1. On Today, tap the **bell** on the **Last feed** card.
2. Choose how long after the **start** of the last feed you want to be reminded.

</div>

The reminder follows the selected baby, so each baby can have their own.

## Pills

In the Google Play version, this tab is called **Habits** and works the same way.

<div class="steps">

1. Open **Pills** and tap **Add pill**.
2. Choose **Times a day** (spread evenly over 24 hours) or **Every few hours**.
3. Set the time of the first dose. Nestling works out the rest, and the schedule restarts at that time each day.

</div>

When a reminder comes up, tap **Taken** to log the dose. Pill reminders only ring on the phone that added the pill, unless you turn them on for another phone too.

## Routines

On a routine, turn on **Remind me on this device**. You get a weekly reminder on the chosen days, with your baby's name. Reminders continue for every baby, even when another one is selected.

## Notification or alarm

How a reminder arrives depends on which version you installed:

- **Google Play:** a notification that plays on the alarm volume, repeats its sound for up to 2 minutes, and has **Stop** and **Snooze** buttons.
- **APK from GitHub:** in **Settings → Reminders** you can choose, separately for feeds and pills, between a notification and an **alarm**. An alarm rings with your alarm tone, shows over the lock screen, and repeats until you stop it (up to 15 minutes). **Snooze** gives you 10 more minutes.

## Make sure reminders arrive on time {#on-time}

Android can hold reminders back to save battery. To avoid that:

- Allow **notifications** for Nestling.
- Allow **Alarms & reminders** for Nestling. Without it, Android may delay reminders by several minutes. **Settings → Reminders** in Nestling asks for it and takes you to the right place.

On Windows, reminders only appear while Nestling is open.
