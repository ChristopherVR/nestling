# Share with a partner

Every phone, tablet and PC in your **family hub** keeps the same log. Connect a device once, and it shares with everyone else in the hub automatically. Nothing goes through the internet: devices talk to each other directly over your home Wi‑Fi.

## Connect a second phone

You need both devices, on the **same Wi‑Fi**, with Nestling open.

<div class="steps">

1. On the phone that **already has your log**, open **Settings → Family hub → Add a device**. It shows a QR code and a 6‑digit code.
2. On the **new** phone, open **Settings → Family hub** and tap **Scan code**. Point it at the QR code.
3. Wait for **Device added and synced**. Both phones now show the same log.

</div>

Setting up a brand-new phone? On the first welcome screen, tap **Joining a partner? Connect their phone** and scan the code from there.

::: tip No camera, or connecting a PC?
Tap **Type code** instead of **Scan code**, and type the **address** and **code** shown on the other device. A Windows PC can show its QR code for a phone to scan, but cannot scan one itself.
:::

## What is shared

Babies, feeds, nappies, sleep, growth, routines and pills, including edits and deletions. **Settings, reminders, the theme and device names stay on each device.**

Each entry remembers who logged it. If you both log the same thing within 10 minutes, Nestling marks it as a possible duplicate so you can delete one.

## Keep syncing in the background

On Android, turn on **Keep syncing when closed** in Settings. Nestling then shows a quiet ongoing notification and keeps your phones in sync even when the app is closed. If Android asks, choose **Allow background use** so it does not pause syncing while the screen is off.

On Windows, sync only runs while Nestling is open.

## Not on the same Wi‑Fi? Sync by QR code {#qr}

Away from home, or on different networks? Use **Sync by QR code**.

<div class="steps">

1. On both devices, choose **Sync by QR code** (also offered as **Not on the same Wi‑Fi? Sync by QR code** when adding a device).
2. One phone shows a series of QR codes; the other scans them.
3. Then they swap, so both end up with everything.

</div>

This also works for phones that have never been paired, and pairs them. Large photo journals may be too big for QR codes; use Wi‑Fi or a [backup file](/guides/backups) for those.

## Troubleshooting {#troubleshooting}

**The other phone is "Not reachable right now".**
Check both are on the same Wi‑Fi and that Nestling is open (or **Keep syncing when closed** is on). Guest networks and some mesh or office networks block devices from seeing each other. Tap **Sync now**; Nestling also looks for phones whose address has changed.

**"Different app versions" or "Paused: different app version".**
Update Nestling on both devices. If you cannot right now, open the device in the hub and tap **Continue anyway**.

**A PC never syncs.**
Allow Nestling through Windows Firewall on **private networks**. If you missed the prompt, allow `baby_app.exe` in **Windows Security → Firewall & network protection → Allow an app through firewall**.

**Typed the wrong code too often?**
After 10 wrong codes in 10 minutes from the same device, Nestling waits 10 minutes before accepting codes from it again.

**Remove a device** from Settings → Family hub. Its copy of the log stays on it until you delete it there.
