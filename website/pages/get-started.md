## Install

<div class="tabs" data-sync="platform" markdown="1">
<section markdown="1">

### Android

1. On your phone, open the [latest release on GitHub](https://github.com/ChristopherVR/nestling/releases/latest).
2. Under **Assets**, tap `Nestling-<version>.apk` to download it.
3. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to **install unknown apps**, then go back and tap **Install**.
4. Open Nestling and allow notifications when it asks, so reminders can reach you.

To update later, see [Backups]({{root}}guides/backups/#updating-nestling).

</section>
<section markdown="1">

### Windows

1. Download `Nestling-<version>-windows-x64.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/ChristopherVR/nestling/releases/latest).
2. Right-click the ZIP and choose **Extract All…**. Extract the **whole** ZIP into a folder you will keep, such as `Documents\Nestling`.
3. Open that folder and run `baby_app.exe`.
4. When Windows Firewall asks, allow access on **private networks**, so your phone can sync with the PC.

Keep `baby_app.exe` together with the DLL files and the `data` folder next to it. You do not need to install anything else.

</section>
</div>

## First-time setup

When you open Nestling for the first time, it asks a few short questions, one per screen. You can skip any of them.

1. **Welcome.** Choose **English** or **Afrikaans** and tap **Get started**. Already using Nestling on your partner's phone? Tap **Joining a partner? Connect their phone** instead, and [connect the two]({{root}}guides/sharing/).
2. **Your baby's name.** A first name or a nickname is fine. Twins? Tap **Add another baby**.
3. **Birthday.** Used to show their age in weeks and months.
4. **A photo** for the Today screen, if you like. Photos stay on your own devices.
5. **How is your baby fed?** Breastfeeding, bottle, or a bit of both. This only decides where a new feed starts; you can always log any kind.

## Something not working?

- **Reminders arrive late.** On Android, allow **Alarms & reminders** for Nestling. See [Habits & reminders]({{root}}guides/habits/#make-sure-reminders-arrive-on-time).
- **Devices do not sync.** Both must be on the same Wi‑Fi, not a guest network. See [Sharing with a partner]({{root}}guides/sharing/#troubleshooting).
- **Anything else?** [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/ChristopherVR/nestling/issues).
