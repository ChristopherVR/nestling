## Export a backup

1. Open **History** and tap the **⋮** menu, or open **Settings → Your data**.
2. Tap **Export backup** and choose where to save the file.
3. Keep it somewhere safe, such as your cloud drive or email.

## Import a backup

Choose **Import backup** in the same menu and pick the file. Importing **adds and updates**; it never deletes anything, so importing the same file twice is harmless.

This is also how you move to a new phone without a family hub: export on the old phone, import on the new one.

## Export a spreadsheet

**Export as spreadsheet** saves the entries that History shows as a CSV file. Filter History first to export only a day or a date range.

## Updating Nestling

<div class="tabs" data-sync="platform" markdown="1">
<section markdown="1">

### Android

Tap **Settings → This device → App version**, then **Update**. Nestling checks the download before installing it. The first time, Android may ask you to allow installs from Nestling; allow it, come back and tap **Update** again.

You can also install a new APK from the [latest release](https://github.com/ChristopherVR/nestling/releases/latest) over the old one. **Do not uninstall first**, or you lose the log on that phone.

</section>
<section markdown="1">

### Windows

Close Nestling, extract the whole new ZIP into a new folder and run it from there. Your log is stored separately and carries over. Windows has no in-app updater.

</section>
</div>

Updates keep your log. After an update, Nestling shows **What's new** once. Devices on different versions pause syncing until they match, so update every device in your family hub around the same time.
