## Add a moment

Add any of these:

- weight in kg
- height in cm
- a photo
- a short memory

Tap a moment to edit or delete it. The journal groups moments by month, shows your baby's age, and charts weight and height by date.

!!! note
    These are your own measurements, not medical percentiles.

## Photos

Photos are resized and kept with your log, so they are included in backups and shared with your family hub. A journal with many photos can be too big to sync by QR code; use Wi‑Fi or a [backup file]({{root}}guides/backups/) instead.
