## History

Search it, filter by type, or pick today, yesterday, a day or a date range, and even one hour of each day. Use the day arrows to step through dates.

Tap any entry to edit or delete it. Deleted something by accident? Tap **Undo**.

## Charts

Tap **Charts** for daily totals (feeds, wees, poos, sleep, feeding minutes, bottle volume and habits) or patterns by hour. Each chart shows the total, the daily average and the busiest day. Tap a bar for details.

A nappy with both counts as a wee and a poo. Today is not finished yet, so its bar is usually shorter.

To save what History shows as a spreadsheet, see [Backups]({{root}}guides/backups/).
