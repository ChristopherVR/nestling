## Feeds

1. Tap **Feed** when a feed starts.
2. Pick **Left**, **Right**, **Both** or **Bottle**. For breastfeeding, Nestling suggests which side is next.
3. Tap **Start timer**, or pick a length and tap **Save** if you are logging afterwards.
4. Need to burp? Tap **Pause to burp**. The timer stops counting until you tap **Resume feeding**.
5. Tap **Stop & save**. Only the actual feeding time is saved, not the breaks.

A running or paused timer survives closing the app. Turn on **Every 5 minutes** for a gentle chime after each five minutes of feeding.

## Nappies

Tap **Nappy**, then **Pee**, **Poo** or **Pee + Poo**. One tap and it is saved.

## Sleep

Tap **Start nap** when you put your baby down, and **End nap** when they wake. You can adjust the start time.

If you open Feed or Nappy during a nap, Nestling asks whether the nap has finished. Choose **Still sleeping** to keep it running.

## More than one baby

Use the baby's name at the top of the screen to switch between babies. The person-plus button adds a baby; the pencil changes their name, birthday or photo. Feeds, nappies, sleep and routines belong to the baby that is selected.

## Fixing a mistake

Open **History** and tap any entry to edit or delete it. Deleted something by accident? Tap **Undo**.

## Night mode

From 6 pm to 6 am the whole app switches to its dark colours by itself, so it is easier on your eyes during night feeds. A small moon shows when this is on.
