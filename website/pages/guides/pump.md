!!! note "Before you start"
    - Pump control works on **Android** only. You can log pumping by hand on any device.
    - It has been tested on the **M5 Pro**. Other Momcozy wearable models may work.
    - This is an unofficial integration, not made or endorsed by Momcozy.

## Connect your pumps

1. On Today, tap **Pump**. Not there? Turn on **Show Pump on Today** in Settings.
2. Switch the pumps on and **close the Momcozy app**: a pump talks to one app at a time.
3. Tap **Find pumps**. When Android asks for **Nearby devices**, allow it, and make sure Bluetooth is on.
4. Tap your pump to connect. If Nestling cannot tell which model it is, it asks you.

Nestling remembers your pumps, so next time they show under **Your pumps**.

## Pump

- Tap **Pump now**. With two pumps, turn on **Both together** to run them as a pair.
- Pick **Massage**, **Expression** or **Mixed**, and use **−** and **+** to set the suction level.
- **Pause** and **Start** as you need.
- Tap **Finish and save**, then add how much you pumped. The M5 Pro cannot measure milk, so you enter the amount.

Prefer not to connect? Tap **Log by hand** to save a session with its time, side and amount.

## Sessions from the pump

Sessions you ran without Nestling can be read from the pump. Open the pump and tap **Check for sessions on the pump**, then add them.

!!! note "Nestling and the Momcozy app share this history"
    Once a pump's history has been read, it is removed from the pump. Whichever app reads it first gets it.

## Trouble connecting?

- Switch the pump off and on, close the Momcozy app, and try again.
- Check that Bluetooth is on and Nestling has the **Nearby devices** permission.
- Still stuck? **Trouble connecting? Open the pump lab** shows details you can include in a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/ChristopherVR/nestling/issues).
