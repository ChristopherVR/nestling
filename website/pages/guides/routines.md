## Add a routine

1. Tap **Add routine**.
2. Give it a name, choose a time and the days of the week, and add notes if you like.

Edit, pause or delete a routine at any time with the pencil.

## Mark it done

Tap **Mark done** to record who did it and when, including something done earlier. Each routine shows when it was **last done**, and **History** lists every time.

## Reminders

Turn on reminders for a routine to get a weekly reminder on the chosen days, on this phone. Reminders include your baby's name and keep coming for every baby, even when another one is selected.

Routines belong to the selected baby and are shared with everyone in your [family hub]({{root}}guides/sharing/).
