## Connect a device

<div class="tabs" markdown="1">
<section markdown="1">

### Same Wi‑Fi

You need both devices on the **same Wi‑Fi**, with Nestling open.

1. On the device that **already has your log**, open **Settings → Family hub → Add a device**. It shows a QR code and a 6-digit code.
2. On the **new** phone, open **Settings → Family hub** and tap **Scan code**. Point it at the QR code.
3. Wait for **Device added and synced**. Both now show the same log.

No camera, or connecting a PC? Tap **Type code** instead, and type the **address** and **code** shown on the other device. A Windows PC can show its QR code for a phone to scan, but cannot scan one itself.

Setting up a brand-new phone? On the first welcome screen, tap **Joining a partner? Connect their phone** and scan the code from there.

</section>
<section markdown="1">

### QR codes, no shared Wi‑Fi

Away from home, or on different networks? Use **Sync by QR code**.

1. On both devices, choose **Sync by QR code** (also offered as **Not on the same Wi‑Fi? Sync by QR code** when adding a device).
2. One phone shows a series of QR codes; the other scans them.
3. Then they swap, so both end up with everything.

This also works for phones that have never been paired, and pairs them. A journal with many photos can be too big for QR codes; use Wi‑Fi or a [backup file]({{root}}guides/backups/) for those.

</section>
</div>

## What is shared

Babies, feeds, nappies, sleep, growth, routines and habits, including edits and deletions. **Settings, reminders, the theme and device names stay on each device.**

Each entry remembers who logged it. If you both log the same thing within 10 minutes, Nestling marks it as a possible duplicate so you can delete one.

## Keep syncing in the background

On Android, turn on **Keep syncing when closed** in Settings. Nestling then shows a quiet ongoing notification and keeps your devices in sync while the app is closed. If Android asks, choose **Allow background use**.

On Windows, sync only runs while Nestling is open.

## Troubleshooting

**The other phone is "Not reachable right now".**
Check that both are on the same Wi‑Fi and that Nestling is open (or **Keep syncing when closed** is on). Guest networks and some mesh or office networks stop devices seeing each other. Tap **Sync now**.

**"Paused: different app version".**
Update Nestling on both devices. If you cannot right now, open the device in the hub and tap **Continue anyway**.

**A PC never syncs.**
Allow Nestling through Windows Firewall on **private networks**. If you missed the prompt, allow `baby_app.exe` in **Windows Security → Firewall & network protection → Allow an app through firewall**.

**Remove a device** in Settings → Family hub. Its copy of the log stays on it until you delete it there.
